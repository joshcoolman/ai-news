## What this is

Personal software for one user: YouTube's subscriptions and Watch Later without the noise. Three parts, Creators, the Player and Favorites (#68); a feature earns its place by being used, not by being interesting, and anything unused for a month goes. A Node server and plain HTML, JS and CSS, with no runtime dependencies and no build step. Every file runs exactly as written: `node server.js`. The reading path is `package.json`, `server.js`, `routes.js`.

## Rules

1. **The outside world goes through one module each:** YouTube `server/youtube.js`, data `server/store.js`. The YouTube module leans on two undocumented playlist ids (its header says which); the store is meant to be swappable.
2. **YouTube is read as little as possible (#67).** The quota is 10,000 units a day and every call costs 1. Creator lists are read at most twice a day (`LIST_TTL_HOURS`) and lengths once ever, all kept in the store's `cache` file so a restart costs nothing. Chapters are parsed from the descriptions those lists already carry; only a video found in no list costs a unit, once. A new call to YouTube is argued in an issue first. The server logs the day's units on every call.
3. **Rules that could silently corrupt the page stay pure and tested** (`server/rules.js`, `browser/shared/pick.js`, `browser/player/queue.js`).
4. **The folder says where code runs.** Only `browser/` is ever served, so nothing in `server/` can reach a page. Neither side imports the other: shapes they share are in `types.d.ts`, numbers they share arrive in `/api/config`.
5. **Every write goes through `store.mutate`,** the cache included.
6. **`dependencies` stays empty and nothing is compiled.** That is the experiment (#21). A new need is written here, in the open, or argued in an issue first. `typescript` and `@types/node` only check the code: `pnpm check` and `pnpm test` are the gate where another repo would run a production build.
7. **A page is one state object and a `draw` function.** Components are functions that return elements built with `h` (`browser/shared/dom.js`); an action changes the state and calls `draw`; lists go through `sync` so unchanged cards are not touched. Attach handlers through `h` or `addEventListener`, not `el.onkeydown = ...`: a property handler that returns false cancels the event, which once swallowed Enter in a search box.
8. **A shortcut is listed where the user can find it.** Keys and clicks that have no button are described in the guide on the Settings page (`GUIDE` in `browser/settings/settings.js`); adding or changing one means updating it there.
9. **Names follow the UI's words.** Glossary:
   - **Card**: one video tile on Creators or Favorites (`browser/shared/card.js`).
   - **Favorite**: a copy of a video on the Favorites page, starred in the Player. Deleting it never touches anything else.
   - **Members only**: a video only a channel's paying members can play. Left out of everything the app shows unless Settings turns that off; the one rule is `playable` in `server/rules.js`.
   - **Player** (Josh says "sidecar"): the one window thumbnails play in (`/player`, named `ainews-player`), with its bar: creator or a green Add creator, a favorite star, and a History column right of the video, always showing, of what the Player has played and what is queued (a new video joins at the top, replaying one keeps the order; each row has a remove ×; removing the one playing moves to the next). Coming back to a video picks it up where it was left. History, finished marks and positions are localStorage: they outlive the window, and a row leaves only by its × (no cap, no expiry, #47). Each opening of the window is a session; an "Earlier" divider sits above the first row an earlier one left (`earlierFrom` in `queue.js`, #48). Never loads youtube.com itself: that wipes the window name and the next click opens a second window. It tells open pages what it changed over a BroadcastChannel.
   - **Queue**: plain-click a video on Creators or Favorites while the Player is playing. The video lands in History right under the one playing; when a video ends, the Player starts the next row not yet watched to the end (`browser/player/queue.js`). The card stays and wears a Queued tag (Playing, for the one playing) for as long as it is in History and ignores further clicks; shift-click takes it out of the queue again (on the playing one, the next plays). No setting, no button. With no Player playing, a click plays. The Player says when it is playing ("playing" on load and in answer to a page's "ping", "closed" on leaving); the Queued marks are read from History itself in localStorage (`browser/shared/history-link.js`), so the Player's own × and Delete, and rows queued before a page opened, count too (#52).
   - **One window** (`/`, `browser/app/`, #60): Creators in a left frame, the Player in a right frame named `ainews-player` (the name thumbnails open into, so a click loads that frame instead of opening a window), a rail between that drags the split (left at least 480px, Player at least 320px; localStorage `app-player-width`). The top bar's Player button (left of Settings, only inside the shell) shows or hides it (localStorage `app-player-shown`); an empty History hides it and disables the button, the first row into an empty History shows it, and nothing else does: queueing or playing while hidden stays hidden (#72). The top bar stays fixed while pages scroll. Shift+Left and Shift+Right give the keyboard to the left half or the Player; F from either half, while the Player shows, fills the display with its video, swallowing every other key, and F again or Escape comes back (`onKey` in `app.js`, #74). The green marks (Highlight, Picked creator, the playing row) show only in the half with the keyboard; the other half keeps them, dark (`--mark`, `browser/shared/away.js`). The window opens with the keyboard on the left, and it goes back there whenever the Player hides (the last row deleted, say). The windowed Player still opens when a page is used on its own (`/creators`). A Player opened with no video (a reload) comes back to the last video it played, else the newest History row, loaded paused (`restore=1`); only an empty History shows the empty message (in the windowed Player; the one window hides it).
   - **Player keys**: bare arrows, Space and Delete, in the Player (`browser/player/player.js`). Space also works on Creators and Favorites outside inputs (`toggleOnSpace`, sent as a `toggle` message; `toggle` in `frame.js` pauses or plays by the tracked state, #61). Up and down play the row above or below in History, stopping at the ends. Left and right skip 10 seconds back or forward. Delete (or Backspace) removes the playing row, as its × does. The video never has the keyboard: the embed takes no pointer (YouTube's controls are dead), a click on it pauses or plays, a double-click or F fills the display (#74).
   - **Centered History**: the playing row sits at the column's vertical middle, a little larger than the rest, and stepping slides the list under it (`history.js` centers it on draw; `player.css` scales it; a view transition morphs the rows across the page load in Chromium browsers).
   - **Highlight**: the card on Creators the keyboard is on, outlined in green (`picked` in `browser/creators/creators.js`; rules in `browser/shared/pick.js`). Left from the sidebar enters the grid at the right-edge card nearest the picked creator's row on screen (`nearestPick`, #58), arrows move it stopping at the edges, Right off its right edge or Escape leaves it, Enter is the card's queue or play (unqueue on a marked card), and changing the creator drops it (#56). A click moves it.
   - **Picked creator**: the creator chosen in the Creators sidebar, outlined in green; the grid shows their whole cached list (the newest 30, `FEED_CAP`) whatever the day slider says and the label reads Latest 30 (#54). While one is picked, up and down move the pick through the list, wrapping; with none picked the arrows do nothing. Clicking it again, or empty page, drops the pick (`browser/creators/creators.js`). C picks the first creator; with one picked, C drops it and goes back to the card highlighted before the first C, else the first card.
   - **Window**: the day slider on Creators, 3 to 14 days (`server/creators/window.js`). Nothing older is shown; a reload reads nothing new, and the status line says when the lists were read.
   - **Chapters**: a button in the Player's bar (and the C key), shown only when the video's description lists chapters; a green C hanging off the lower-left corner of a card or History row thumbnail says it does (`chapters` on a `Card`, read from the cached description in `server/cards.js`; History rows from before the dot ask `/api/player/chaptered` once). It opens a list over the History column; picking one jumps there and the list stays open until ×, Escape or a click elsewhere; while it is open, up and down walk the chapters instead of the videos (`browser/player/chapters.js`, `parseChapters` in `server/youtube-parse.js`).
   - **Add your key**: the first screen when the server has no key of its own (`browser/shared/keys.js`). The key lives in this browser's local storage and rides each request as a header; Settings has Delete key.
