Find new things in AI that someone who builds with AI tools would want to click on.

- **A thing that now exists.** A model, a tool, a product, a demo, an open-source project. Something the reader could try or look at.
- **Recent.** Days old, not weeks. If there is little that is new, return little.
- **What it does, not what people think of it.** No opinion pieces, reaction threads or rankings.
- **No alarm.** Nothing framed as a threat, a scandal or a warning; nothing that would run on the evening news as an AI scare story.
- **Not business news.** No funding rounds, acquisitions, lawsuits, regulation or platform policy.
- **Label:** one to three words naming the specific thing ("Dots", "Decisions API", "Dots clone"). No numbers. No adjectives.
- **Title:** one plain sentence saying who made it and what it does. A little more information than the label, nothing clever. Do not lead with prices or benchmark scores.
- **Source:** the maker's own page (announcement, docs, repository) when one exists; a news write-up only when it does not. Open the page before you cite it.
- The creator video titles you are given show what is currently worth covering. Use them to choose topics, including things the creators have not covered yet.
